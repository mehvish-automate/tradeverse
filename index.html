1. Executive Summary

TradeVerse is a pure skill-game for the Indian stock market — a Duolingo-shaped daily habit combined with Dream11-shaped social competition, built on top of live NSE/BSE market data.

!

We are not a trading app.

We never touch real money. No brokerage, no KYC for markets, no demat, ever.

We compete with Mathiks, Kahoot, Stepn — not with Zerodha, Groww, or eToro.

Year-1 revenue is deliberately zero. V2 monetization is B2B white-label seats to colleges, CA institutes, and finfluencer communities. V3 adds skill-gaming tournaments (separate legal entity). V4 adds US/UAE markets for the NRI corridor.

2. Problem Statement

  • 15Cr+ Indian demat accounts, yet most retail users remain passive, intimidated, and under-educated about markets.
  • Existing apps split cleanly into two flawed categories:
    • Cockpits (Zerodha, Groww) — for people who already know what they're doing.
    • Gambling-adjacent (Dream11-for-stocks, paid prediction contests) — regulatorily radioactive and ethically indefensible.
  • There is no daily-habit, social, skill-first, regulation-safe product for the 22–28 year-old first-jobber who is curious about markets but won't open a demat account.

3. Vision & Positioning

3.1 Vision

Make pattern-recognition, chart-reading, and market-fundamentals a daily 5-minute skill game that Gen Z Indians play with their friends.

3.2 Core USP

For first-job Gen Z Indians (22–28) who find the stock market intimidating, TradeVerse is the only markets platform that turns live market data into a daily visual skill game and friend-league — no trading, no real money — unlike Zerodha (a cockpit for the initiated), Groww (a storefront), or Dream11 (gambling dressed up as sport).

3.3 One-liner

"Mathiks for markets, Duolingo for instinct, WhatsApp for the social spine."

4. Target Users

4.1 Primary Persona — "Rohan, the Curious First-Jobber"

  • 22–28 years old
  • ₹30K–₹80K/month income
  • Tier 1 / Tier 2 Indian city
  • Has heard of Zerodha, hasn't opened an account, or opened one and abandoned it
  • Spends 40+ min/day on Instagram + WhatsApp + Dream11
  • JTBD: "I want to feel like I understand the stock market without feeling like an idiot or losing money."

4.2 Secondary Persona — "Ananya, the Campus Enthusiast"

  • 18–22, college student (IIT/NIT/IIM/top B-school/commerce college)
  • Member of Finance/Investment Club
  • No income; time-rich, social-active
  • JTBD: "I want to win something with my friends and look smart doing it."

4.3 Explicit non-personas

  • Active retail traders (Zerodha's user) — not us.
  • Passive long-term SIP investors (Groww's user) — not us.
  • Under-18 users — gated by age at signup.

5. Goals & Non-Goals

5.1 Goals

  1. Build the most habit-forming markets app in India (D30 retention ≥ 30%).
  2. Establish a system-generated content moat that scales with every trading day.
  3. Ship a regulation-safe product that no SEBI circular can touch in V1.
  4. Create a B2B-ready white-label platform by V2 for colleges and institutes.

5.2 Explicit Non-Goals Locked

6. Hero Offerings

# Hero What it is Why it matters
H1 Daily Chart Challenge 5–10 system-generated visual questions per day, derived from yesterday's NSE/BSE data. Patterns, charts, S/R, fundamentals. Answered in 3–5 min. Scored on accuracy + speed. D30 retention engine + press moment ("I learned chart reading in 10 days") + regulation-safe core.
H2 Friend Leagues (Skill-Based) 3–20 friends compete weekly on the same generated challenges. Leaderboard by accuracy + speed. Zero money. The virality engine. WhatsApp-native, India-flavored, zero regulatory exposure.

8. Content Taxonomy (The Moat)

Six problem classes, all system-generated from public market data. Every trading day yields ~3,500 stocks × 6 classes = ~21K fresh problem seeds.

# Class Input Example Difficulty scales via
1 Pattern Recognition OHLCV candlesticks "Identify the pattern in this chart" Noise, subtlety
2 Chart Matching OHLCV, multiple stocks "Which is Reliance from last Tuesday?" Sector-similar distractors
3 Indicator Reasoning RSI / MACD / Bollinger "Rank these 4 by RSI" Close numeric values
4 Fundamentals Decode Quarterly filings "Match these 4 P&L statements to companies" Similar-size companies
5 News → Market Logic News + same-day price "Which headline drove this 7% move?" Same-day distractors
6 Microstructure V3 L2 order-book "Is buy or sell pressure higher?" Balanced books

V1 covers classes 1–3. V2 adds 4–5. V3 adds 6.

·

Engine is system-generated for MVP. Hire 1 quant/analyst in V2 to tune problem difficulty, catch edge cases (splits, circuit-breakers, corporate actions).

16. UX Principles

Surface Style Principle
Play (challenges, leagues, streaks, clans) Bright, colorful, sound, confetti Reduce friction, maximize dopamine.
Reflection (analytics, journal, report card) Muted, data-dense, honest Productive friction — force self-assessment.
·

Invariants

  • No financial advice, ever. Disclaimer on every market-adjacent screen.
  • Honest feedback — tell users where they were wrong, not just "great job."
  • Visual-first; text is minimal (≤12 words per question screen).
